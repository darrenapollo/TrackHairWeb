# Track Hair Product Marketing Context

## Product

Track Hair is an iOS hair loss tracking app for people managing hair loss routines over months. It helps users keep treatment schedules, progress photos, regimen notes, and visible change history in one place.

## Primary Audience

People who are tracking hair loss, thinning, shedding, regrowth, transplant recovery, or long-term hair density changes. The audience includes men and women, and the website should not frame hair loss as a male-only problem.

## Core Problem

Hair loss changes slowly, and people often judge progress from memory, daily mirror checks, inconsistent photos, or scattered treatment notes. That makes it hard to tell whether a routine is being followed, whether visible change is happening, or what changed in the regimen.

## Positioning

Track Hair is a focused hair loss treatment journal, not a generic habit tracker. It is built around the specific workflows people need when monitoring hair loss: treatment consistency, repeatable photos, long review windows, and regimen history.

## Core Value Proposition

Track your hair loss routine with enough structure to judge progress over time.

## Key Benefits

- Keep treatment schedules and reminders in one place.
- Save repeatable progress photos for long-term comparison.
- Document dose, frequency, notes, shedding, and routine changes.
- Track multiple treatments together, including minoxidil, finasteride, microneedling, PRP, red light therapy, and transplant recovery.
- Use AI-assisted visible change tracking as an informational reference, not a diagnosis.

## Differentiation

Generic habit apps can remind users to do something, but they do not understand the context of hair loss. Track Hair connects reminders, photos, and treatment history so users can review progress with more context than memory alone.

## Primary CTA

Download on the App Store

## Secondary CTAs

- Explore treatment guides
- Read the hair loss guide
- Browse the treatment library

## Voice and Tone

Clear, practical, medically careful, and supportive. The copy should feel calm and credible, not dramatic or fear-based. Use plain language that people searching for hair loss help would naturally use.

## Claims and Safety

Avoid guaranteed regrowth claims, diagnostic claims, and overpromising AI analysis. Make it clear that Track Hair is a tracking tool, not a medical service. Treatment decisions should be made with a qualified clinician, especially for prescription medication, pregnancy risk, procedures, or unexplained shedding.

## Copy to Avoid

- Male-only positioning such as "for men" unless the page is specifically about male pattern hair loss.
- Overly broad claims such as "stop hair loss" or "regrow your hair."
- Sensational fear copy about balding.
- Medical certainty where diagnosis or clinician guidance is needed.
- Generic productivity language such as "streamline," "optimize," or "unlock your potential."

## Preferred Language

- Hair loss tracking
- Progress photos
- Treatment schedules
- Routine consistency
- Regimen notes
- Visible change over time
- Pattern hair loss
- Male and female pattern hair loss
- Long-term review
- Track changes with context

## SEO Focus

Primary topics include hair loss tracker app, hair loss tracking, hair progress photos, minoxidil tracking, finasteride tracking, hair transplant recovery tracking, hair loss treatment routine, male pattern hair loss, female pattern hair loss, and androgenetic alopecia.

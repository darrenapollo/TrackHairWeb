## What red light therapy is

Red light therapy, sometimes grouped under low-level light therapy, uses light exposure with the goal of supporting hair growth. In hair loss discussions it is usually positioned as a device-based option that may complement a broader treatment plan.

## How it may work

The proposed mechanisms center on light effects at the cellular level, but the practical takeaway for users is simpler: it is not a treatment category that should be judged casually or instantly.

## Who it may suit

It may appeal to people who want a non-drug, device-based option to discuss with a clinician. Whether it is worth the time and cost depends on expectations, diagnosis, and the rest of the plan.

## Typical timeline

| Window | What to track |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Session frequency and adherence |
| Early months | Whether the schedule is realistic enough to maintain |
| Longer review | Photo-based comparisons rather than short-term impressions |

## Side effects and risks

Short-term use appears relatively well tolerated, but medical sources still note that evidence varies by use case and more research is needed. Avoid treating marketing claims as settled fact.

## How to track red light therapy with Track Hair

1. Add the device routine with the exact weekly schedule you intend to follow.
2. Log actual session completion rather than the ideal plan.
3. Keep photo comparisons on a monthly or similarly structured cadence.
4. Track it alongside the rest of your regimen so you can judge the full plan rather than one device in isolation.
